# Customer Interview Guide (Phase 0)

**Product:** Elba (علبة), custom printed packaging with a free 3D designer and vetted Egyptian print vendors.
**Purpose:** validate Phase 0 assumptions in `docs/research-report.md` section 10 (steps 1 and 6) before we spend on Phase 1.
**Target:** 15 to 20 interviews in weeks 0 to 4. Split: 6 to 8 Instagram/TikTok D2C brands, 3 to 4 F&B (bakeries, cloud kitchens, cafes), 3 to 4 cosmetics/personal care, 3 agencies or freelance designers.
**Format:** 30 to 40 minutes, in person or video call (WhatsApp video is fine). Record only with explicit consent (see the consent script below).

---

## 1. What we need to learn (hypotheses)

| # | Hypothesis | Evidence that confirms it | Evidence that kills it |
|---|---|---|---|
| H1 | SMEs order custom packaging in runs of 100 to 2,000 units and are poorly served today | Last order was 100 to 2,000 units, or they wanted custom but quit because of minimums | Most buy stock boxes and are happy; or always order 5,000+ |
| H2 | Getting a quote is slow and painful (WhatsApp, days of back and forth) | Quote took 2+ days, needed 3+ messages, prices inconsistent | Quotes come same day from a trusted printer |
| H3 | Seeing the box in 3D before paying reduces fear of a bad run | Past mistakes (wrong colour, logo upside down, wrong size); wants to "see it first" | Never had a problem; trusts the printer's PDF |
| H4 | They will pay online upfront (card, wallet, InstaPay, Fawry) for a first order | Already prepay suppliers; would prepay with a proof and guarantee | Insists on cash on delivery only |
| H5 | They will give a phone number to get an instant price and a mockup file | Says yes without hesitation; already shares WhatsApp with suppliers | Refuses to share a number before a price |
| H6 | Price within roughly +10% of their current printer is acceptable for speed, proof and guarantee | Names a willingness range that leaves our margin room | Will only switch for a lower price |
| H7 | Agencies and designers will use a free 3D tool for client presentations and refer orders | Currently fake mockups in Photoshop; would welcome a referral commission | Already use Pacdora or paid mockups and are satisfied |

## 2. Screening criteria

Ask these before booking. Book the interview only if the person passes.

**Must have (all):**
- Decision maker or direct influencer on packaging purchases (owner, co-founder, ops lead, brand manager, or the agency lead who specifies packaging).
- Business operating in Egypt, selling at least monthly.
- Bought, or seriously tried to buy, custom or printed packaging in the last 12 months, or plans to in the next 3 months.

**Segment fit (at least one):**
- D2C brand selling via Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, own website (Shopify, WooCommerce, Zid, EasyOrders) or marketplaces.
- F&B business with delivery or takeaway (bakery, cloud kitchen, pizza, desserts, cafe).
- Cosmetics, skincare, perfume or candles producer.
- Branding agency or freelance designer who has designed packaging for at least one client.

**Exclude:**
- Large FMCG with procurement departments ordering 50,000+ units (not our wedge; note them as a later lead).
- Packaging printers or resellers (interview them with the vendor guide instead).
- Friends and family of the team.

**Screening message (WhatsApp, Arabic):**

> مرحبا [الاسم]، أنا [اسمك] من فريق علبة. نعمل على منصة لطباعة علب الكرتون المخصصة بكميات صغيرة مع معاينة ثلاثية الأبعاد قبل الطلب. نرغب في محادثة مدتها 30 دقيقة لنفهم تجربتك مع شراء العلب والتغليف. لا نبيع شيئا في هذه المحادثة. هل اشتريت علبا مطبوعة لعلامتك خلال آخر 12 شهرا؟ وهل يناسبك موعد هذا الأسبوع؟ شكرا لك.

**Incentive:** a free physical sample (1 to 3 pieces, digital print) of their own design when we launch, or EGP 300 to 500 voucher on the first order [ASSUMPTION]. Do not offer cash.

## 3. Consent script (read before starting)

**English:** "Thank you for your time. We are researching how businesses in Egypt buy packaging. There are no right or wrong answers. With your permission I will take notes and record audio only so I don't miss anything. The recording stays with our team, is not shared, and will be deleted within 90 days. You can stop at any time or ask us to delete your answers. May I record?"

**العربية:** "شكرا لوقتك. نحن ندرس كيف تشتري الشركات في مصر علب التغليف. لا توجد إجابات صحيحة أو خاطئة. بعد إذنك سأدون ملاحظات وأسجل الصوت فقط حتى لا يفوتني شيء. التسجيل يبقى لدى فريقنا ولن نشاركه مع أحد، وسنحذفه خلال 90 يوما. يمكنك التوقف في أي وقت أو طلب حذف إجاباتك. هل توافق على التسجيل؟"

Log the answer (yes/no, date, time) in the note template. This is our PDPL 151/2020 consent record for research data.

## 4. The 20 questions

Rules for the interviewer: ask about past behaviour, not opinions about the future. Follow every answer with "Tell me more" or "Why?". Do not pitch until question 18. Do not show the 3D demo before question 17.

### Part A: Context (warm up)

| # | English | العربية |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Tell me about your business: what you sell, where, and roughly how many orders a month. | حدثني عن نشاطك: ماذا تبيع، وأين، وكم طلبا تقريبا في الشهر؟ |
| 2 | How do your products reach the customer today, and what are they packed in? | كيف يصل منتجك إلى العميل اليوم، وفي أي تغليف؟ |
| 3 | What role does packaging play for your brand? Is it protection only, or part of the experience? | ما دور التغليف بالنسبة لعلامتك؟ هل هو للحماية فقط أم جزء من تجربة العميل؟ |

### Part B: Last purchase (the core; get specifics)

| # | English | العربية |
|---|---|---|
| 4 | Walk me through the last time you bought printed or custom packaging. When was it and what triggered it? | احك لي عن آخر مرة اشتريت فيها علبا مطبوعة أو مخصصة. متى كان ذلك وما السبب؟ |
| 5 | What exactly did you order? Box type, size, material, number of colours, finish, quantity. | ماذا طلبت بالضبط؟ نوع العلبة والمقاس والخامة وعدد الألوان والتشطيب والكمية. |
| 6 | How did you find the printer, and how many did you contact? | كيف وجدت المطبعة، وكم مطبعة تواصلت معها؟ |
| 7 | How long from first message to final price? How many back-and-forth messages? | كم استغرق الوقت من أول رسالة حتى السعر النهائي؟ وكم رسالة تبادلتم؟ |
| 8 | What did you pay in total and per unit? Did that include the die (اسطمبة التكسير), plates, delivery, VAT? | كم دفعت إجمالا وللقطعة الواحدة؟ وهل شمل ذلك اسطمبة التكسير والزنكات والتوصيل وضريبة القيمة المضافة؟ |
| 9 | How did you pay: deposit, full upfront, on delivery? Cash, transfer, InstaPay, wallet? | كيف دفعت: عربون أم كامل المبلغ مقدما أم عند الاستلام؟ نقدا أم تحويل أم إنستاباي أم محفظة؟ |
| 10 | How long did production and delivery take compared with what you were promised? | كم استغرق الإنتاج والتوصيل مقارنة بالموعد المتفق عليه؟ |
| 11 | How did you see and approve the design before printing? (PDF, photo, physical sample, nothing) | كيف رأيت التصميم ووافقت عليه قبل الطباعة؟ (ملف PDF، صورة، عينة، لا شيء) |
| 12 | Did anything go wrong: colour, size, print position, damage, delay? What did it cost you and how was it resolved? | هل حدثت مشكلة: في اللون أو المقاس أو مكان الطباعة أو تلف أو تأخير؟ كم كلفتك وكيف تم حلها؟ |

### Part C: Pains, alternatives, priorities

| # | English | العربية |
|---|---|---|
| 13 | What is the most frustrating part of buying packaging today? | ما أكثر شيء مزعج في شراء التغليف اليوم؟ |
| 14 | Have you ever wanted custom boxes but did not order? Why not (minimums, price, time, design)? | هل أردت علبا مخصصة ولم تطلبها؟ لماذا (الحد الأدنى، السعر، الوقت، التصميم)؟ |
| 15 | Rank what matters most when choosing a printer: price, quality, speed, minimum quantity, reliability, ease of ordering. | رتب ما يهمك عند اختيار مطبعة: السعر، الجودة، السرعة، الحد الأدنى للكمية، الالتزام، سهولة الطلب. |
| 16 | How often do you reorder, and what would make reordering easier? | كم مرة تعيد الطلب، وما الذي يجعل إعادة الطلب أسهل؟ |

### Part D: Concept reaction (only now show the demo)

| # | English | العربية |
|---|---|---|
| 17 | (Show the 3D mailer box demo on your phone.) What is your first reaction? What would you do next on this screen? | (اعرض نموذج العلبة ثلاثية الأبعاد على هاتفك.) ما انطباعك الأول؟ وما الخطوة التالية التي ستقوم بها على هذه الشاشة؟ |
| 18 | To see the price and download the mockup, we ask for your mobile number with a code. Would you do that? Why or why not? | لعرض السعر وتنزيل صورة التصميم نطلب رقم الموبايل مع رمز تحقق. هل ستفعل ذلك؟ لماذا؟ |
| 19 | If the price were [their last unit price +10%] with a 3D proof, a vetted printer, tracking and a reprint guarantee, would you pay online in full upfront for a first order? Which method? | لو كان السعر [سعر القطعة السابق + 10%] مع معاينة ثلاثية الأبعاد ومطبعة موثوقة وتتبع للطلب وضمان إعادة الطباعة، هل ستدفع كامل المبلغ أونلاين مقدما لأول طلب؟ وبأي وسيلة؟ |
| 20 | Is there anything we did not ask that we should know? Who else should we talk to? Can we contact you when we launch? | هل هناك شيء لم نسأل عنه ويجب أن نعرفه؟ من تنصحنا بالحديث معه؟ وهل يمكننا التواصل معك عند الإطلاق؟ |

### Segment add-ons (pick 1 or 2, replace Q16 if short on time)

- **F&B:** Do your boxes touch food directly? Has any supplier given you a food-safe certificate? / هل تلامس العلب الطعام مباشرة؟ وهل قدم لك أي مورد شهادة صلاحية للتعامل مع الأغذية؟
- **Cosmetics:** Do you need retail-shelf cartons (SBS) as well as shipping boxes? Any barcode or regulatory text requirements? / هل تحتاج علبا دوبلكس للعرض في المتاجر بجانب علب الشحن؟ وهل توجد متطلبات باركود أو بيانات إلزامية؟
- **Agencies/designers:** How do you present packaging to clients today? Would you share a 3D link with a client? Would a referral commission on orders change how you work? / كيف تعرض تصميم العلب على عملائك اليوم؟ هل سترسل رابط معاينة ثلاثية الأبعاد للعميل؟ وهل تغير عمولة الإحالة على الطلبات طريقة عملك؟
- **Seasonal:** Did you buy special packaging for Ramadan, Eid or New Year? When did you start ordering? / هل اشتريت تغليفا خاصا لرمضان أو العيد أو رأس السنة؟ ومتى بدأت الطلب؟

## 5. Closing commitment test (the real signal)

At the end, ask for one of these, in order, and log which they accepted:

1. **Pre-order:** "We can produce your next run in [month]. Would you pay a deposit now to reserve it?" (strongest)
2. **LOI:** sign a one-page non-binding letter of intent with quantity and target price.
3. **Artwork:** send their logo/artwork so we prepare a 3D mockup and real quote within 48 h.
4. **Intro:** introduce us to another brand owner.
5. **Waitlist:** join the WhatsApp waitlist (weakest).

Phase 0 decision gate (report section 10, step 6): at least 30 qualified leads, at least 5 prepaid orders or signed LOIs.

## 7. After the interviews: synthesis

- Within 24 h, the note taker cleans the notes and fills the hypothesis scores.
- Each week, put all quotes on a shared board grouped by hypothesis. Count supports vs contradicts per segment.
- Feed every real price you hear into `reference-jobs-rfq.md` (market price column) and the rate card spreadsheet.
- Delete recordings on the date in the note header. Keep only anonymised notes after 90 days.
