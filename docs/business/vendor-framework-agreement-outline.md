# Vendor Framework Agreement: Outline (اتفاقية إطارية مع المطابع)

> **Not legal advice. A qualified Egyptian commercial lawyer must draft and finalise this agreement** under the Egyptian Civil Code and Commercial Law No. 17 of 1999, check enforceability of the non-solicitation, penalty and exclusivity clauses, and produce the binding **Arabic** version (Arabic prevails in Egyptian courts). This outline lists the commercial terms we want so the lawyer can draft efficiently. Numbers marked [ASSUMPTION] are our proposals for negotiation.

**Parties:** [Elba company legal name], an Egyptian LLC, commercial register no. ____, tax card no. ____ ("Elba"); and [Vendor legal name], commercial register no. ____, tax card no. ____ ("the Vendor").
**Structure:** a framework agreement (اتفاقية إطارية) plus individual **Job Orders** (أوامر تشغيل) issued through the Elba vendor portal or WhatsApp-confirmed PDF. Each accepted Job Order is a separate supply contract governed by the framework.

---

## 1. Purpose and relationship
- Elba sells printed packaging to its customers in its own name, as seller and merchant of record. The Vendor manufactures for Elba as a subcontracted supplier.
- The Vendor has **no contract with Elba's customers** and does not invoice them.
- Non-exclusive for both parties unless a separate capacity reservation is signed (see 10).
- Independent contractors; no agency, partnership or employment.

## 2. Definitions
Job Order, Approved Proof (the 3D + PDF proof approved by the customer), Print-Ready File, QC Photos, Lead Time, Working Day (Sunday to Thursday excluding official holidays) [confirm], Defect, Reprint, Customer, Elba Customer (any customer introduced through Elba).

## 3. Job Orders and acceptance
- Elba issues a Job Order with: structure code, inner size, material, colours, sides, finish, quantity, Print-Ready File, Approved Proof, buy price, lead time, delivery address.
- Vendor accepts or declines within **4 working hours** [ASSUMPTION]. Silence = declined; Elba may route to another vendor.
- Price in the Job Order is the price on the Vendor's current rate card (with the agreed index). The Vendor may not re-price an accepted Job Order.
- Over/under delivery tolerance: **+/- 3%** of quantity [ASSUMPTION]; invoice for actual delivered quantity within tolerance.

## 4. Rate card and price changes
- Vendor provides a rate card in Elba's template (`rate-card-template.csv`), valid for at least **90 days** [ASSUMPTION].
- Board/FX price changes: Elba applies a published index; the Vendor may request a rate card update with **14 days' notice** and supporting evidence (mill price list). Accepted Job Orders keep their price.
- Rate cards and Elba's sell prices are confidential (see 13).

## 5. Quality standards
- Production to the Approved Proof and the Print-Ready File. No changes to artwork, colours, size or material without written approval from Elba.
- Tolerances [ASSUMPTION, confirm with vendors]: cut and crease within +/- 1 mm; print registration within 0.3 mm; colour within agreed tolerance of the proof (Delta E 2000 <= 5 on brand colours where measured, otherwise visual match under D50 light); board grade as specified; no visible scuffs, set-off, delamination or crushed flutes.
- Food-contact jobs: only on materials and inks for which the Vendor holds current evidence (supplier declarations, migration test report; NFSA Decision 17/2022 compliance). The Vendor must tell Elba immediately if a certificate lapses.
- Elba may audit the site with **48 hours' notice** and run test jobs.

## 6. QC photos and release
- Before dispatch, the Vendor uploads to the Elba portal: (a) the first approved printed sheet, (b) at least 3 finished boxes (closed front, open, side), (c) the packed bundles with labels showing the Job Order number and count.
- Elba approves or rejects within **1 working day** [ASSUMPTION]. Goods may not leave the factory before approval.
- Approval of photos does not waive Elba's rights for hidden defects.

## 7. Lead time and delivery
- Lead time per Job Order, counted in Working Days from Elba's acceptance confirmation and file release.
- Late delivery: Vendor notifies Elba as soon as it knows of a risk of delay. Liquidated damages of **2% of the Job Order value per Working Day late, capped at 20%** [ASSUMPTION; lawyer to confirm enforceability under the Civil Code], unless the delay is caused by Elba or force majeure.
- Delivery by Vendor van, Elba's courier (Bosta-type pickup) or customer pickup, as stated in the Job Order. Packing: flat, in labelled bundles protected from moisture.
- Risk passes on handover to Elba's courier or on delivery where the Vendor delivers.

## 8. Defects and reprints
- Elba (or the customer) reports defects within **30 days** of delivery (aligned with the 30-day defect remedy under Consumer Protection Law 181/2018 [lawyer to confirm]).
- Vendor-caused defects (deviation from Approved Proof, specification or tolerances): the Vendor **reprints at its own cost** within the original lead time, or refunds the Job Order price at Elba's choice, and bears the delivery cost of the reprint.
- Customer-caused errors that appear in the Approved Proof (typos, wrong logo) are not Vendor defects.
- Disputes on whether a defect exists: joint inspection within 3 Working Days; if unresolved, an independent print expert agreed by both parties decides, losing party pays the expert.
- Elba keeps a reprint reserve; the Vendor's reprint rate is a KPI (target below 3%).

## 9. Payment terms
- Vendor issues an **ETA e-invoice** to Elba for each Job Order (B2B e-invoice is mandatory). No e-invoice, no payment.
- Proposed schedule [ASSUMPTION]: **40% advance** within 2 Working Days of Job Order acceptance, **60% balance** within 7 days of QC photo approval and confirmed delivery.
- Payment by bank transfer to the Vendor's company account only (no personal accounts, no cash).
- Elba may set off amounts owed for reprints, liquidated damages or refunds against later payments, with a written statement.
- Prices ex-VAT; VAT added on the e-invoice at the applicable rate (14%).

## 10. Capacity and priority
- Vendor states monthly capacity for Elba jobs. Before Ramadan, Eid al-Adha and Q4, Elba may reserve capacity with a non-refundable reservation fee or minimum volume commitment [optional, negotiate].
- Vendor gives Elba **14 days'** notice of planned shutdowns.

## 11. Non-solicitation and non-circumvention
- During the agreement and for **12 months** after it ends [ASSUMPTION; lawyer to confirm reasonable scope and duration], the Vendor will not, directly or through related parties, solicit or accept orders for packaging from any Elba Customer whose identity it learned through Elba, unless the customer was already a documented customer of the Vendor before the first Elba Job Order (a pre-existing customer list may be annexed).
- No marketing material, business cards, phone numbers, website or QR codes may be placed in or on goods shipped to Elba Customers.
- Vendor may not contact customers; all communication goes through Elba.
- Remedy: liquidated damages equal to [X]% of the estimated value of diverted orders or a fixed amount per breach [lawyer to set], plus termination.
- Elba will not solicit the Vendor's employees for 12 months.

## 12. Intellectual property and files
- Customer artwork, logos and the Print-Ready Files remain the property of the customer/Elba. The Vendor uses them only for the Job Order and deletes them on request or after **12 months**.
- Cutting dies paid for by Elba or the customer are **Elba's property**, stored by the Vendor free of charge, labelled, and released to Elba within 5 Working Days on request.
- The Vendor must refuse or flag any job that looks like counterfeit or infringing branding.

## 13. Confidentiality and data protection
- Prices, rate cards, customer identities, volumes and files are confidential during and for 3 years after the agreement.
- The Vendor processes customer personal data (names, phones, delivery addresses on labels) only as Elba's **processor** under PDPL 151/2020 and its Executive Regulations: only for delivery, kept secure, not shared, deleted after the job, breach notice to Elba within 24 hours so Elba can meet the 72-hour PDPC deadline. Annex a short data processing schedule.

## 14. Vendor profile and portfolio
- The Vendor permits Elba to publish its name, city, machines, capabilities, certifications, ratings, on-time %, lead time and portfolio photos (shot by Elba) on the Elba site.
- Ratings come from customer reviews and Elba's QC data; the Vendor may respond to reviews through Elba.

## 15. Compliance
- Vendor maintains a valid commercial register, tax card, VAT registration, industrial/operating licences, labour and social insurance compliance, and health and safety at the site.
- No child labour; safe working conditions (Elba may refuse to work with sites that fail a basic safety check).
- Anti-bribery: no gifts or payments to Elba staff.

## 16. Liability and insurance
- Vendor liable for direct losses caused by its breach, capped at [the value of the Job Order / 12 months of fees], except for confidentiality, non-solicitation, IP breaches and food-contact non-compliance (uncapped or higher cap) [lawyer to set].
- Vendor keeps fire and property insurance for stock and stored dies [recommended].

## 17. Term and termination
- Term: 12 months, auto-renewing for 12 months unless either party gives 30 days' notice.
- Termination for cause: repeated quality failures (e.g. reprint rate above 8% over 3 months), on-time delivery below 85% over 3 months, breach of non-solicitation or confidentiality, loss of required licences.
- On termination: finish accepted Job Orders (or refund advances), return dies and files.

## 18. Force majeure
Standard clause (Civil Code article 373 concept): power cuts beyond agreed duration, government action, natural events. Board shortages and price rises are **not** force majeure.

## 19. Governing law and disputes
Egyptian law. Amicable negotiation for 15 days, then [Cairo Regional Centre for International Commercial Arbitration (CRCICA) / Cairo Economic Courts] [lawyer to choose]. Arabic is the binding language.
