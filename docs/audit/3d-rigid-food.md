# 3D audit and fixes: rigid boxes, paper tubes, food service

Key: `3d-rigid-food` · Branch: `claude/exciting-albattani-8kzgsu-3d-rigid-food` · Base: head of `claude/exciting-albattani-8kzgsu`.
Scope: `src/lib/dieline/families/rigid-tubes`, `src/lib/dieline/families/foodservice` and their tests. Shared engine and library problems are written up in §3 and not edited.

## Method

| Step | How |
|---|---|
| Models | The 22 library models these folders put in the designer (`/[locale]/design`, wired through `src/lib/library/boxes.ts`): 8 food service (cake box, bakery box, burger clamshell, noodle pail, fries scoop, sandwich wedge, pizza slice, round pizza) and 14 rigid / tube / bulk models (7 rigid set-up boxes, 6 paper tubes, pallet box). The folders also hold models the library leaves out (moulded pulp, cups, lids, bags, envelopes, octabin, pallet box with lid). Those were checked by geometry only. |
| Renders | Production build, `/en/dev/3d?model=…` (the designer's `DesignScene` → `Scene3D` path), Chromium + SwiftShader, 1x DPR, reduced motion. Each model shot closed (iso), closed (back and underside), at 50 % fold, and flat. Harness: the `3d-visual` audit's `capture.cjs`. Contact sheets are in `docs/screenshots/audit/3d-rigid-food/`, before and after. |
| Geometry | A closed-pose surface check now runs as a unit test (`rigid-tubes/__tests__/surfaces.ts`) on every preset of every board, with and without options. It flags **z-fighting** (two faces in one plane, facing the same way, overlapping by 20 mm² or more), **piercing** (two pieces whose board slabs overlap, such as a lid through a base), and **artwork orientation** (1 mm right or down in a face must move along that face's right or down vector in 3D, so a mirrored or rotated part fails). |
| Audit input | The findings for these families in `docs/audit/3d-visual.md` (branch `…-audit-3d-visual`, §7 FR-01…FR-09 plus the cross-cutting X-06, X-07 and X-10) are merged into the table below. |

Severity: **critical** = broken model or flow; **high** = clearly wrong to a packaging buyer; **medium** = noticeable realism or polish defect; **low** = nitpick.
