# 3D audit: FEFCO 06/07 (bliss boxes, ready-glued cases, crash-lock bottoms) and FEFCO 09 (interior fittings)

Scope: `src/lib/dieline/families/fefco-06-07` (33 models) and `src/lib/dieline/families/fefco-09` (48 models, 36 of them in the live designer through `src/lib/library/boxes.ts`).

## Method

1. **Geometry sweep (Node).** For every model, every preset, the corners of its limits and calipers 0.5 / 1.5 / 3 / 4 mm: closed bounds against the declared outer size, fitting bounds placed in the host (`placement.matrix`), and every pair of parallel panels checked for **shared board volume**: slabs that overlap by more than 5 % of a caliper over more than 4t² + 20 mm² in plan. Those pairs z-fight, or one board shows through another, in the 3D view. Checked closed and at 50 % fold. The check is now a permanent test (`fefco-09/__tests__/overlap.ts`, used by both suites).
2. **Live designer (Playwright).** Every model in the library from these families (69: 33 + 36) opened at `/en/design/<slug>` on a production build, 1280 × 860, screenshots of the 3D stage at **Closed** and **Half**. The same run was repeated after the fixes. Contact sheets are in `docs/screenshots/audit/3d-fefco-06-09/`.
3. Cross-checked against `docs/audit/3d-visual.md` (branch `…-audit-3d-visual`): its F67-01…07 and F09-01…05 are folded in below.

### Checked and correct (no finding)

- **Closed dimensions.** For all 33 FEFCO 06/07 models, the closed size equals `outerSize(dims, t)` at 1.5 and 3 mm for every preset and limit corner. The bliss box body is L + 4t long with stitched body flaps and L + 2t without. The crash-lock bottom stacks 3t.
- **Fold order.** The crash-lock stages follow the erecting sequence (tube squared, side flaps, lock flaps with the front outermost). On bliss boxes the walls go up, then the ends move in, then the body or end flaps, then the lid.
- **Normals.** On every model the printed face points out of the closed box, except glue tabs and stitched end flaps (unprinted, inside). No panel is inside out.
- **Fit.** All 48 fittings sit inside the host at 0.5 to 4 mm with no slack, after FF-01 to FF-03.
- **Flute edge.** The board caliper comes from `BOARD_SPECS` (1.5 mm E, 3 mm B). The cut-edge flute texture is shared (3d-visual X-05).
