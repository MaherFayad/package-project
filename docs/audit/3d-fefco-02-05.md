# 3D audit: FEFCO 02 (slotted) and 03 / 05 (telescope, slide) models

Key: `3d-fefco-02-05` · Branch: `claude/exciting-albattani-8kzgsu-3d-fefco-02-05` · Scope: `src/lib/dieline/families/fefco-02`, `src/lib/dieline/families/fefco-03-05` and their tests.
Before / after contact sheets: `docs/screenshots/audit/3d-fefco-02-05/` (`before-02a…05.png`, `after-02a…05.png`; columns: closed 3/4, back, under, top, half fold, flat) and `options-board-and-inside-print.png` (0202, 0217, 0301, 0510: other board zoomed on the edge, and print-both-sides at half fold). In `before-03c.png` the octabins show plain board because the first harness run's test design exceeded the 400 pt font limit and was rejected; the bare corner panels were confirmed on the corrected run before the fix.

## Method

1. **Renders in the live designer.** Every library model of the two families (56: the library serves `FEFCO_0201` and `FEFCO_0320` from the core engine, so those two are out of scope) was opened at `/en/design/<code>` with a test-pattern design injected through the designer's own autosave (`?resume=1`). The pattern puts a coloured panel, a diagonal, a circle and the face name with a right arrow ("FRONT R→") on every face, so a seam, a stretch, a mirrored face or an unprinted panel is visible at a glance. Six views per model: closed 3/4, back (180°), under, top, half fold, flat. Chromium + SwiftShader, 1x DPR, `reducedMotion: reduce`.
2. **Geometry probes on the closed and half-folded box** (now permanent tests, `fefco-02/__tests__/closedPose.ts`): each panel is sampled on a 6 mm grid at mid-board and on both surfaces, and every sample is tested against every other panel's slab. That finds board passing through board (*penetrate*), coincident same-facing surfaces (*z-fight*), the share of each face that is covered by board (gaps / unprinted strips), and face transforms that mirror or scale artwork. Run at 1.5 mm (E flute) and 3 mm (B flute).
3. **Reference drawings.** Sandland Packaging, *FEFCO Codes brochure v3* (2024), which reproduces the FEFCO 11th-edition drawing of every code: checked 0207, 0216, 0217, 0218, 0225, 0226, 0227, 0228, 0229 and 0322 page by page. The FEFCO PDF mirror at umbudagerdin.is refused the connection, so the brochure was the drawing source.
4. Findings from the report-only session (`docs/audit/3d-visual.md` on `claude/exciting-albattani-8kzgsu-audit-3d-visual`, sections 2 and 3) were re-checked against the renders and probes and are listed with their ids.

| Low | 5 | 4 | 2 (3d-visual F35-04, F35-06) | 1 (hinge edge strip → engine E-1) |

All closed models now pass at their default size: no interpenetration, no z-fighting, every face covered and unmirrored, at 1.5 and 3 mm (one documented exception: the 0308 gusset web, where the board really bends round the corner). A sweep of every preset and size-limit corner is clean except three hidden contacts, left open on purpose: the 0308 / 0309 inside-gusset web bend at the long wall (real bend), and the 0226 bottom gusset tip touching the end wall's bend at 3 mm (inside the closed box, never visible).

## Engine and library issues (not edited: owned by other sessions)

| id | sev | file | issue | suggested fix |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| E-1 | low | `src/components/three/panelGeometry.ts · buildPanelGeometry` | Side (cut-edge) quads are built on **crease hinges** too. | E-2 | medium | `src/lib/dieline/fold.ts · computeFaces` | Every panel with `stackOffset < 0` is skipped, even when it is visible beside the outermost flap. | E-3 | medium | `src/lib/dieline/fold.ts · panelLocalPoint` | `stackOffset` moves a panel's mesh but not its children, so a stacked panel with children (shelf + division, centre + gussets) leaves them a caliper off. | E-4 | medium | `src/lib/dieline/fold.ts · assemblyPoseAt` | Extra blanks (lid, caps, inner sleeves, liners) lerp straight from the sheet to their pose with a sine lift, so octabin bottom caps (0351 / 0352) and 05 inner sleeves (0504 / 0505 / 0507) pass through the body half-way (3d-visual X-09). | Three-phase path: rise above the body (`lift`), travel over the target, then descend / slide along the piece's open axis. |

| L-1 | medium | `src/lib/library/boxes.ts · familyModels` | FEFCO 02 `build` never passes style params, and `DesignDoc` has no field for them, so overlap o, frame h, tray h, liner h and bevel angle are fixed at their defaults in the designer. | Add `styleParams` to `DesignDoc` and pass `params` through `build` (README integrator step 4). |
