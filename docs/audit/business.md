# Business audit: running product vs the plan (round 2)

**Key:** business · **Scope:** report only, no source changes · **Plan:** `docs/research-report.md` §0, §1.3, §4, §6, §7, §8, §10 · **Date:** 2026-11-06

**Method.** `npm run build` + `next start` on a freshly seeded SQLite DB. I walked each plan item in Chromium (Playwright) at 1440×900 and 390×844, in `en` and `ar`, as an anonymous visitor, a buyer (`+201000000002`), vendor staff (`+201000000010`) and an admin (`+201000000001`). I called `/api/quote` directly and read the code paths behind every screen. Screenshots: `docs/screenshots/audit/business/`. The one data change (a vendor bio carrying a phone number and URL, used to test disintermediation) was reverted afterwards and confirmed gone from the public page.

**Status legend:** done · partial · missing · contradicts (the product does the opposite of the plan). **Impact** is business impact: critical = money, legal exposure or the marketplace model is at risk; high = a funnel bridge or plan control is broken; medium = it weakens conversion or trust; low = polish.

## 3. What works well (keep)

- **G2 gate**: reason-specific copy, phone and OTP only, the user's intent resumed after sign-in, anonymous drafts claimed. This is the plan's §1.3 pick, done properly.
- **Merchant-of-record mechanics**: Elba prices from vendor buy-price rate cards plus admin margin; buyers never see buy prices (public offer fields are sell-side only); vendors see a first name and a city; Elba arranges the courier; webhooks are the only path to PAID; payouts wait for the vendor's e-invoice.
- **Print-file lock**: the PDF exists only for paid orders, and for vendors only after they accept the job. The proof PDF is watermarked.
- **PDPL product controls**: versioned, provable consent; withdrawal; export; delete; an admin data-request queue; essential-only cookies.
- **Admin pricing**: margin, validity, zones and the FX/board index, with a preview of the change before saving, and audit-logged.

## 4. Not verifiable in the product (founder actions from §8 and §10)

PDPC licence and DPO appointment (grace period ended around 31 Oct 2026); VAT registration and seller tax registration number (`ELBA_TAX_ID` is unset); ETA accredited provider contract; Paymob marketplace compliance letter; NFSA food-contact certificates per vendor (the profile supports a `FOOD_SAFE` certificate with document upload and expiry check); vendor non-solicit clause.
